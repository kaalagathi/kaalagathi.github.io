Registration & Zoom guide

Follow these simple steps to register for a Kaalagathi course, complete the required details, and join the live online class. Menu options are in English; course learning content is in Telugu.

01

Choose your course

Open the Home page, choose the course you want, and read the Telugu course description, current fee, batch dates and class timings.

Browse all courses ↗
02

Submit the registration form

Open the registration link shown on the relevant course page. Enter your details carefully and submit the form. Keep a note of the name and phone number you registered with.

Marriage Astrology: Open registration form ↗

03

Install Zoom

Use the official Zoom website or app store for your device. Install Zoom Workplace on your computer or mobile phone.

Download Zoom ↗
04

Sign up or sign in

  • If you are new to Zoom, open Zoom and choose Sign Up. Follow the verification instructions.
  • If you already have an account, choose Sign In and enter your login details.
  • You may usually join a meeting from an invitation link without creating an account, unless the host requires sign-in.
Zoom sign-in ↗
05

Complete payment only after confirmation

Confirm the current course fee, offer deadline, payment method and recipient with the Kaalagathi team before paying. Save your transaction reference. Do not rely on an old promotional message for current pricing.

06

Join your live class

  • Look for the Zoom meeting link and class instructions from the course team.
  • Open the link a few minutes before class begins.
  • Allow microphone/audio access as needed. Keep your display name recognizable.
  • Do not share the private class link publicly.
Important: This website does not currently process payments or automatically send Zoom invitations. The Kaalagathi team should confirm registration, payment and meeting details with each student. Never share your Zoom password or one-time verification code.
